# Users

- **Front Desk:** answers calls, finds patient records and schedules appropriate appointments. Often interrupted; preserve work across steps.
- **Dentist:** needs appointment reason, urgency and a compatible operatory, with realistic gaps between bookings.
- **Dental Assistant:** prepares the operatory and reviews appointment type and patient alerts.
- **Clinic Manager:** coordinates capacity across locations. Reassignment is outside this exploration.

Primary persona: front-desk coordinator at Northside, working on a desktop while speaking with a patient.
