# Constraints

- Preserve existing clinic navigation during this exploration.
- Appointment type determines duration: emergency assessment 30 minutes, urgent treatment 60 minutes.
- Provider and operatory availability must be checked for the full duration.
- Emergency appointments visibly identify urgency.
- Front-desk workflows assume desktop usage; Patient Search also explores a mobile viewport.
- Use only fictional records. No diagnosis, medical triage recommendation, clinical eligibility decision or actual appointment is made.
- Sample availability is on 6 October 2026, at Northside; this fixed date is intentional, not live data.
